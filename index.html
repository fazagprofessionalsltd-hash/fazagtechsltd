Company Profile

Fazag Techs Ltd is a technology company that designs, builds and supports business software for organizations of different sizes and sectors. We turn day-to-day operations such as accounting, payroll, human resources, projects, patient records and customer portals into simple, connected digital systems.

Our solutions are built as progressive web apps, so they run in any browser, can be installed on a phone like a native app, and stay usable on modest internet connections.

We work closely with each client: understanding their processes first, then configuring, training and supporting their teams so the technology delivers real results.
